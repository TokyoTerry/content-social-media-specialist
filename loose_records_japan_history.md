# Bridging the Underground: The Story of Loose Records Japan

In the global landscape of underground electronic music, few movements capture raw, high-energy synergy quite like the cross-continental bridge built between Portugal and Japan. At the heart of this niche subculture sits **Loose Records Japan**, an independent underground electronic music label established in 2013 that has spent over a decade anchoring the full-on psychedelic trance (psytrance) scene in Tokyo.

### The Architect: Full Propulsion
The musical foundation of Loose Records Japan belongs to Riu, the mastermind behind the psytrance project **Full Propulsion**. Originally formed as a collaborative project in Portugal, Full Propulsion shifted into a solo endeavor in 2011 when Riu relocated to Japan. 

Bringing the aggressive, intricate production styles of Western European psytrance to the Far East, Riu utilized Full Propulsion as both a performance alias and a creative sandbox. His sonic signature—characterized by driving basslines, complex synthesis, and high-energy arrangements—is perfectly captured in landmark releases like his *Source* EP. 

### The Operator: Loose Cannon
Upon arriving in Japan's hyper-competitive electronic circuit, Riu crossed paths with **Loose Cannon**, an underground electronic music promoter and artist. Recognizing a gap in the market for uncompromising, authentic full-on psytrance, the duo teamed up in 2013 to officially launch Loose Records Japan. 

While Riu guided the label's sonic identity, Loose Cannon took the reins of the business infrastructure. Serving as the primary coordinator, Loose Cannon managed the label's promotional pipeline, handled artist scouting, and developed a platform designed to elevate both established international producers and rising local talent. Together, the duo established the label as a cultural gateway, connecting the European and Asian underground electronic communities.

### Conquering the Tokyo Underground
Before Loose Records Japan even pressed its first release, the groundwork was laid on the dancefloors of Tokyo. From 2010 to 2012, Full Propulsion operated **Psy.co**, a highly active underground party series. These events became legendary within the local subculture, routinely billing Riu alongside global psychedelic trance titans such as GMS, Ace Ventura, Talamasca, Mad Maxx, CPU, and Absolum. 

Following the official launch of the label in 2013, the founders pivoted from standalone parties to curated label showcases. These showcases acted as a launchpad for Loose Records roster talent, most notably showcasing prominent Japanese producers like **Masaki (LOOP / Loose Records)**. 

The label’s events quickly found permanent homes in Tokyo's most iconic underground venues:
* **Koenji CAVE:** A definitive sanctuary for Tokyo's sub-genre electronic scenes, where the founders and label roster frequently anchored the renowned *Epoch Making* event series.
* **Shibuya LOOP:** A multi-floor nightlife staple where the duo hosted expansive events, effectively bridging the geographical and stylistic gaps between Tokyo's progressive electronic fans and the full-on psytrance community.

### A Lasting Electronic Legacy
Through a dedicated catalog of releases, international radio broadcasts with platforms like Psychedelik.com, and a relentless event schedule, Loose Records Japan solidified its footprint in electronic music history. By blending Loose Cannon’s promotional vision with Full Propulsion’s uncompromising Portuguese production roots, the label remains a testament to the power of independent, cross-cultural underground music.
