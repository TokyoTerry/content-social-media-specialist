# Loose Records Japan

**Loose Records Japan** is an independent underground electronic music record label founded in Tokyo, Japan, in 2013. The label was co-established by Portuguese psychedelic trance (psytrance) producer **Full Propulsion** (Riu) and underground music promoter and artist **Loose Cannon**. The label specializes in high-energy, "full-on" psychedelic trance, characterized by driving basslines and complex synth production, serving as a cultural pipeline between European and Japanese underground electronic music circuits.

## History and Overview

Prior to the label's formal establishment, Riu operated a solo psytrance project under the moniker Full Propulsion. Originally formed as a collaborative group in Portugal, Full Propulsion became Riu's solo vehicle following the departure of other members in 2011. That same year, Riu relocated to Tokyo, where he began organizing underground electronic music events.

In 2011, Riu launched **Psy.co**, a Tokyo-based underground party series that ran until 2012. The events featured prominent international and domestic psychedelic trance acts, including GMS, Ace Ventura, Talamasca, Mad Maxx, CPU, and Absolum. 

In 2013, Riu partnered with Tokyo-based artist Loose Cannon to found Loose Records Japan. Under the partnership, Full Propulsion managed the label's musical direction, production quality, and core repertoire, while Loose Cannon oversaw business operations, international promotional strategy, and artist scouting.

## Roster and Releases

Loose Records Japan focuses primarily on underground electronic music, specifically full-on psytrance and progressive sub-genres. The label provides a platform for both international producers and local Japanese artists. 

### Notable Artists
* **Full Propulsion (Riu):** Co-founder and core producer.
* **Loose Cannon:** Co-founder, artist, and promoter.
* **Masaki:** A prominent Japanese producer associated with the label and the Tokyo venue LOOP.
* **DJ Muffy:** An underground DJ and artist aligned with the label's promotional showcases.

### Key Releases
The label's catalog includes digital extended plays (EPs) and compilations, highlighted by Full Propulsion's solo EP titled *Source*. The label has also broadcasted content through international electronic music platforms, including collaborations with the online radio network *Psychedelik.com*.

## Event Promotion and Venues

Loose Records Japan regularly curates label showcases and club nights to promote its roster within the Kanto region's nightlife scene. The label has maintained recurring residencies and events at several notable Tokyo electronic music venues:

* **Koenji CAVE:** A fixture of Tokyo's sub-genre electronic scene, where the label routinely hosted acts under the *Epoch Making* event banner.
* **Shibuya LOOP:** A multi-floor venue utilized by the label to host cross-genre events, bridging the local progressive electronic and full-on psytrance communities.
