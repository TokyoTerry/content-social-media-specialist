# Cover Letter Template

**P1** opens on the single strongest match and immediately lists the posting's own
responsibility verbs in one sentence. No throat-clearing.

**P2** widens to adjacent experience that makes the P1 claim credible, then sweeps
up the nice-to-haves in a short clause.

**P3** is one line. No summary, no recap of qualifications.

**Sign-off** is plain. Contact details only.

## Anti-patterns

- "I am writing to express my interest in..." — delete.
- "I am excited about the opportunity to..." — delete.
- Restating the job title back at the reader in the first sentence.
- Three consecutive sentences opening with "I".
- Em dashes doing the work commas should do.
- A closing paragraph that summarizes the letter that just happened.
- Enthusiasm adjectives standing in for evidence ("passionate", "thrilled",
  "deeply committed").

## Long-form variant

Only when the user asks for a sentence per responsibility. Structure:

1. Two-sentence opening establishing total relevant experience.
2. One body paragraph per responsibility cluster, each sentence naming a real
   employer and a concrete artifact or outcome.
3. Short paragraph covering explicitly-requested extras the user listed.
4. One-line closing.

Report the resulting word count and offer to cut it to the 150-word standard.
