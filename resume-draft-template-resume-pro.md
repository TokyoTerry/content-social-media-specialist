## Phase 4: ATS Optimization (Pre-Formatting Audit)

- [ ] Font: Consistent 12px throughout
- [ ] Sections: Standard order (Contact | Summary | Experience | Education | Skills)
- [ ] Dates: "Month Year – Month Year" format, consistent
- [ ] No graphics, tables, colored backgrounds, embedded images
- [ ] Bullet points: Action verb + responsibility + outcome (quantified if possible)
- [ ] Keywords: 3–5 from posting naturally integrated (not keyword-stuffed)
- [ ] Spacing: Adequate white space (not cramped, not sparse)
- [ ] Length: 1 page (default) / 2 pages (if 20+ yrs experience justified)
- [ ] Education: Single-line format — *Degree, University, start date – end date*
- [ ] No "I/me" language; passive/active voice appropriate to field

## Phase 6: Cleanup & Finalization

### Proofreading Checklist
- [ ] Spelling: No typos, consistent capitalization
- [ ] Grammar: Parallel structure in bullet points, correct tense usage
- [ ] Consistency: Font size, spacing, formatting uniform throughout
- [ ] Tone: Professional, outcome-focused, no buzzwords

### AI-Writing Tells to Remove
- [ ] "I believe," "I think," "I feel" → State directly
- [ ] Vague qualifiers: "highly," "very," "quite" → Remove or be specific
- [ ] Overused phrases: "passionate about," "dynamic," "innovative" → Replace with evidence
- [ ] Repetition: Say once, move on (no echoing of skills)
- [ ] Filler: Remove padding sentences; trim to essentials

### Final Review
- [ ] Does resume match job posting language/focus?
- [ ] Does resume represent T accurately and confidently?
- [ ] Is tone consistent with terry-writing-style-emulation?
- [ ] Ready to submit?
